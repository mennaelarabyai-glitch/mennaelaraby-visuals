    About

    Where creative direction meets AI production

    I’m Menna Elaraby, an AI video specialist with more than three years of experience in video and visual content.

    I help brands, businesses and content creators turn their message into short films that grab attention and get people to act. I handle the whole project myself: the idea, the script, the AI scenes, the edit and the motion graphics. That keeps the look consistent and the message clear from the first frame to the last.

    From product launches and ad campaigns to explainer videos and social media content, I adapt the style of each film to the brand and its audience, in any industry.
